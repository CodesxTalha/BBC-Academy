Our Programs

Karate

Traditional Shotokan and Olympic-style Karate for all ages — kids (boys and girls) and adults. Master Kihon fundamentals, traditional Kata forms, and full-contact Kumite sparring under WKF-certified instruction with official Dan belt gradings.

KIDS & ADULTS • ALL AGES 60 MIN / SESSION MON — SAT AFTERNOONS WKF BELT GRADING

Boxing

Precision punch mechanics, evasive head movement, footwork, and counter-striking combinations. Covers foundational pad drills, heavy bag conditioning, and technical ring sparring with dedicated female and male tracks.

BEGINNER TO PRO 60 – 90 MIN / SESSION GIRLS & OPEN SESSIONS TECHNICAL PAD WORK

Kickboxing

Dutch and American kickboxing systems trained under WAKO-certified methodology. High-volume combinations synchronizing rapid hand strikes with low kicks, body roundhouses, and clinch knees across Senior and Junior tracks.

JUNIOR & SENIOR TRACKS 75 – 90 MIN / SESSION MON • WED • FRI SESSIONS WAKO CERTIFIED

Mixed Martial Arts (MMA)

Our flagship combat discipline. Seamlessly blends stand-up striking, clinch takedowns, cage wall wrestling, and ground submissions under international IMMAF standards for self-defense and fight readiness.

FLAGSHIP SYSTEM 90 MIN / SESSION MON • WED • FRI (7:30 PM) IMMAF COMPLIANT

Fitness & Weight Loss Training

High-intensity combat metabolic conditioning designed to torch calories, shed fat, and forge athletic endurance. Incorporates bag circuits, bodyweight calisthenics, and core complexes with separate female and male class slots.

HIIT & WEIGHT LOSS 60 MIN / SESSION FEMALE & MALE SLOTS ALL FITNESS LEVELS

Sword Training (Katana) & Weapons

Traditional Japanese Kenjutsu blade mastery, Katana handling, solar weapons defense, tactical disarming, and multi-weapon disciplines. A rare and revered martial discipline in Pakistan taught with strict safety protocols.

TRADITIONAL KENJUTSU 60 – 90 MIN WEAPONS DISARMING RARE IN PAKISTAN
