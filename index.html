About BBC Academy

Built on Discipline. Proven in Combat.

Founded as an elite martial arts training institution in the Twin Cities, BBC Academy is dedicated to the pursuit of authentic combat mastery, physical resilience, and unshakeable self-discipline. We bridge traditional martial arts values—respect, honor, and mental toughness—with modern sports science and high-intensity combat conditioning.

Our programs are designed and supervised by certified black belts and federation coaches with decades of championship ring and mat experience. From technical boxing combinations, Dutch kickboxing drills, and Olympic Karate kumite, to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu transitions, cage wall wrestling, and traditional Japanese Katana blade defense, our systematic curriculum builds complete, adaptable practitioners.

Whether you are an absolute beginner taking your first steps toward self-defense, an individual pursuing athletic fat loss and functional conditioning, or an aspiring fighter preparing for regulated competition, our mats offer a disciplined, supportive proving ground where every student is pushed to reach their full potential.

We believe martial arts is more than physical combat—it is a lifelong practice that instills focus, integrity, and composure under pressure. Inside our academy, warriors are forged through sweat, continuous repetition, and mutual respect.

/ OUR PROGRAMS

FIND YOUR PERFECT FIT.

There is no age limit. We have sessions available for every age group. We offer karate classes for kids (boys and girls), and for adults we provide mixed martial arts, fitness, and self-defense training.

KARATE

Traditional Shotokan and Olympic-style Karate for all ages — kids (boys and girls) and adults. Master Kihon fundamentals, traditional Kata forms, and full-contact Kumite sparring under WKF-certified instruction with official Dan belt gradings.

KIDS & ADULTS • ALL AGES 60 MIN / SESSION MON — SAT AFTERNOONS WKF BELT GRADING

BOXING

Precision punch mechanics, evasive head movement, footwork, and aggressive counter-striking combinations. Covers foundational pad drills, heavy bag power conditioning, and technical ring sparring with dedicated female and male tracks.

BEGINNER TO PRO 60 – 90 MIN / SESSION GIRLS & OPEN SESSIONS TECHNICAL PAD WORK

KICKBOXING

Dutch and American kickboxing systems trained under WAKO-certified methodology. High-volume combinations synchronizing rapid hand combinations with low kicks, body roundhouses, and clinch knees across Senior and Junior / New divisions.

JUNIOR & SENIOR DIVISIONS 75 – 90 MIN / SESSION MON • WED • FRI (6 – 9 PM) WAKO CERTIFIED

MIXED MARTIAL ARTS (MMA)

Our flagship combat discipline. Seamlessly blends stand-up striking, clinch takedowns, cage wrestling, and ground submissions under international IMMAF standards. Designed for real-world self-defense and amateur or pro fight readiness.

FLAGSHIP COMBAT SYSTEM 90 MIN / SESSION MON • WED • FRI (7:30 PM) IMMAF COMPLIANT

FITNESS & WEIGHT LOSS TRAINING

High-intensity combat metabolic conditioning designed to torch calories, shed fat, and forge functional stamina. Incorporates bag circuits, bodyweight calisthenics, and core complexes with separate dedicated female and male fitness class slots.

HIIT & WEIGHT LOSS 60 MIN / SESSION FEMALE & MALE SLOTS ALL FITNESS LEVELS

SWORD TRAINING (KATANA) & OTHER WEAPONS

Traditional Japanese Kenjutsu blade mastery, Katana handling, solar weapons defense, tactical disarming, and multi-weapon disciplines. A rare and revered martial discipline in Pakistan taught with strict safety protocols and elite focus.

TRADITIONAL KENJUTSU 60 – 90 MIN WEAPONS DISARMING RARE IN PAKISTAN
