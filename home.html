Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about joining, classes and what to bring.

Is there an age limit to join BBC Academy?

No. We run classes for every age: kids' and youth karate, plus karate, boxing, kickboxing, MMA, weapons and fitness programs for adults.

Are there separate classes for women and girls?

Yes. Women-only classes include Fitness & Self-Defense (weekdays, 1:30 – 2:30 PM), Girls & Kids Karate, Girls Youth Karate, Adult Girls Karate & Self-Defense, and Girls Boxing (Tue · Thu · Sat). See the schedule above for exact times.

Do I need prior martial arts experience?

No. Coaches start with stance, footwork, breathing and safety before moving on to pad work or sparring, so you can begin at any level.

How does the free first class work?

Call or message us on WhatsApp at +92 336 5279129 to reserve a slot. Wear comfortable gym clothes and bring water. You'll train with a coach, see the academy, and decide afterwards with no commitment.

What gear do I need to bring?

For your first class, comfortable athletic clothing and a water bottle are enough. After you enroll, uniforms (gi), gloves, hand wraps and shin guards can be arranged through the academy.

Ready to get started?

Your first class is free. Step onto the mats, meet the coaches and start training today.
